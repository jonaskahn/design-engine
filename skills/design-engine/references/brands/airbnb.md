# Airbnb
Source: https://www.airbnb.com/ (observable brand/product design; brand resources)
Character: Warm, human, photographic. Emphasizes real places and people over graphic decoration.
Poor fit for: developer documentation, industrial tools
Palette roles: surface `#FFFFFF`, text `#222222`, muted `#717171`, accent rausch/pink-red `#FF385C`, dark `#222222`, soft highlight `#FFF0EC`
Typography: Airbnb Cereal (proprietary). Open fallback: Nunito Sans, Public Sans. Friendly humanist sans with generous body size.
Shape & space: 8–12px rounded cards/images; 4px spacing base; photography-first large imagery with soft corners
Motion: gentle; subtle scale on hover for cards, smooth image crossfades
Signature moves: photo-forward rounded cards, pill-shaped search/booking bar, warm red accent, large friendly headings
Licensing: Airbnb Cereal font files are proprietary.
Slop overlap: pill-shaped controls (deliberate here), rounded cards
