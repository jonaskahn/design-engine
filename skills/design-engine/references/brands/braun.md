# Braun (Dieter Rams design principles)
Source: https://www.braun.com/ , and Dieter Rams "Ten Principles for Good Design" (attributed via official Braun/Vitsoe/Rams foundation sources)  ·  Tokens verified: 2026-10 (model knowledge; re-verify at lock-in) against https://www.braun.com/
Status: principles are timeless; brand-site design is updated. Use as a *philosophy* reference more than a token set.
Character: "Weniger, aber besser" — less, but better. Unobtrusive, honest, useful, and long-lived. A strong antidote to decorative AI defaults.
Best for: personal, marketing, ecommerce, application-ui (as a restraint discipline)  ·  Poor fit for: playful/social products, expressive campaigns
Palette roles (observed): white `#FFFFFF`, off-white `#F5F5F5`, black `#000000`, one restrained accent (Braun orange `#F05A28`-family or green detail), neutral gray
Typography: clean neutral grotesk (e.g. Braun's own face, proprietary). Open fallback: Helvetica Neue → Inter, Public Sans. Functional, unfussy, high legibility.
Shape & space: simple geometric forms, square to slightly rounded; plain surfaces, no gratuitous decoration
Motion: minimal, only what clarifies function
Signature moves: single accent used sparingly on monochrome, clear grid, plain surfaces, one obvious primary control, honest materials
Do not copy: Braun logo, product imagery, trademarked typeface files
Slop overlap: none (explicitly the antidote; use as a rejection filter on decorative choices)
