# Teenage Engineering
Source: https://teenage.engineering/ (observable product/site design)
Character: Playful industrial. Bright blocks of color, hardware-like controls, naive typography, zero corporate polish.
Poor fit for: enterprise SaaS, government, fintech
Palette roles: white/off-white base, black, and a set of saturated accents (orange, green, blue, yellow, red) used as physical-looking blocks; no gradients
Typography: a plain, slightly naive grotesk (verify licensing). Open fallback: Inter, Space Grotesk — note Space Grotesk is a slop-default; only use if Teenage Engineering is explicitly locked.
Shape & space: square/low-radius, chunky blocks, visible grid; colorful segmented control-like elements
Motion: limited; snapping, mechanical transitions
Signature moves: solid saturated color blocks, outlined button/segment styles, dot-matrix and knob-like UI metaphors, stark white ground
Licensing: Product photography and the faces in it are licensed assets; product names are trademarked.
Slop overlap: none (this system is the opposite of the usual slop palette)
