# Figma
Source: https://www.figma.com/ (observable brand/product design; Design System for marketing)
Character: Confident creative-tool energy. Colorful and expressive in marketing, precise and compact in the product canvas.
Poor fit for: regulated government, understated luxury
Palette roles: canvas `#FFFFFF`, dark canvas `#1E1E1E`, text `#0D0D0D`/`#FFFFFF`, blue `#0D99FF`, purple `#9747FF`, red `#F24822`, green `#14AE5C`, orange `#FFCD29`
Typography: Inter (product), Figma's own display faces for marketing (proprietary). Open fallback: Inter, Public Sans. Compact 11–13px UI labels.
Shape & space: 3–6px radius, dense 4px spacing grid; visible alignment/spacing indicators are part of the product language
Motion: fast, precise, cursor-responsive; minimal decorative animation
Signature moves: multi-color layer/selection colors, floating contextual toolbars, dark canvas with light inspector panels, precise grid/ruler overlays
Slop overlap: Inter (legitimate here), multi-color accents
