# Atlassian Design System
Source: https://atlassian.design/ (foundations: color, typography, spacing, components)
Character: Productive and confident, tuned for dense multi-product work tools like Jira and Confluence. Approachable without being playful.
Poor fit for: luxury branding, editorial magazines
Palette roles: surface `#FFFFFF`, surface-sunken `#F7F8F9`, text `#172B4D`, text-subtle `#626F86`, selected/blue `#0C66E4`, text-success `#216E4E`, text-danger `#AE2E24`, dark `#1D2125`
Typography: Atlassian Sans / Atlassian Serif (newer); formerly Charlie Display/Charlie Text. Open fallback: Inter, Public Sans. 12/14/16px base ladder with 24/29/32px headings.
Shape & space: 3px small radius, 6px cards; 8px spacing base; elevation through shadow and border, not tone alone
Motion: short, fast easing; emphasize state change over movement
Signature moves: blue selection surfaces, product-level left nav with collapsible sections, "spotlight" onboarding emphasis rings, red destructive buttons, structured empty states
Licensing: Charlie typeface files are proprietary.
