Shape & space: 6–8px radius; generous section spacing on marketing, dense tables in dashboard; gradient-tinted section backgrounds
Motion: subtle scroll-linked gradient shifts and delicate hover states; restrained
Signature moves: deep navy ink on white, vivid blurple CTA, gradient-tinted sections, precise hairline cards, code-sample panels
