# Apple (Human Interface Guidelines + apple.com)
Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/ , https://www.apple.com/ (observe marketing)
Verify at lock-in: the current material language (Liquid Glass, 2025).
Character: Restrained, physical, and content-forward. Systems built on real materials, precise typography, and deference to the user's task.
Poor fit for: dense data tools, playful social
Palette roles: system background `#FFFFFF` / `#000000`, secondary background `#F2F2F7`, label `#000000`/`#FFFFFF`, secondary label `#3C3C4399`, blue `#007AFF`, green `#34C759`, red `#FF3B30`, orange `#FF9500`
Typography: SF Pro / SF Pro Display / SF Pro Text (Apple platforms only, proprietary). Open fallback: Inter, Public Sans. Tight display tracking; text sizes scale with Dynamic Type.
Shape & space: 4–10pt radii, continuous/curved corners; 8pt spacing base; depth through translucency and light, not heavy shadow
Motion: spring physics (bouncy, interruptible); fluid transitions; strong reduced-motion support
Signature moves: translucent layered materials, large title navigation bars, system-metric spacing, segment controls, haptic-feeling feedback states
Licensing: SF Pro font files are licensed for Apple platforms only; iOS status bar chrome is Apple’s.
Slop overlap: pill shapes, dark-only (deliberate on dark surfaces only)
