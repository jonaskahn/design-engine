# The Verge
Source: https://www.theverge.com/ (observable editorial site design)
Character: Loud, confident tech journalism. Big type, strong color blocking, and an unmistakable point of view.
Poor fit for: enterprise tools, regulated services
Palette roles: black `#000000`, white `#FFFFFF`, accent hot pink/magenta `#E2219B`-family, yellow highlights, bold border blacks
Typography: a proprietary heavy display sans paired with a text face (Poly Sans / custom, licensing unclear). Open fallback: Archivo Black or Space Grotesk for display (note Space Grotesk is a slop default — use only if The Verge is locked), Public Sans for body.
Shape & space: square/low-radius, thick black rules and borders, dense editorial grid
Motion: bold hover treatments, minimal decorative animation
Signature moves: thick black keylines, saturated accent blocks, oversized headline type, category chips
Licensing: Proprietary font files.
Slop overlap: none (though the type is bold in a way unrelated to AI slop defaults)
