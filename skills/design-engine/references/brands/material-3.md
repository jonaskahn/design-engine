# Material Design 3 (Google)
Source: https://m3.material.io/ (styles: color, typography, shape, elevation, motion; Figma Material Theme Builder)
Verify at lock-in: Material 3 Expressive (2025) or standard — Expressive adds larger shape morphs and springier motion.
Character: Systematic and accessible by construction: tonal palettes derived from a seed color, so color roles stay consistent across light and dark.
Poor fit for: editorial magazines, luxury branding
Palette roles: derived from a seed. Light: surface `#FEF7FF`-class tone 98, on-surface tone 10, primary tone 40, on-primary tone 100. Dark: surface tone 6 (~#141218), surface-container tone 12/17/22, primary tone 80, on-primary tone 20. Use Material Theme Builder to generate exact hexes.
Typography: Roboto Flex / Roboto (open, Apache), Roboto Serif optional. Type scale: Display, Headline, Title, Body, Label with small/medium/large.
Shape & space: shape scale 0–28px (extra-small 4 → full circle); 4/8dp spacing base; tonal elevation replaces white-overlay shadow
Motion: springy expressive easing (`emphasized` 500ms), container transforms; supports reduced motion
Signature moves: tonal surface containers (surfaceContainerLowest→Highest), pill-shaped filled buttons, large rounded navigation bars, dynamic color from a seed
Slop overlap: pill-shaped buttons, dark-only, tinted dark surfaces (deliberate here; tonal roles are not the "purple gradient" tell)
