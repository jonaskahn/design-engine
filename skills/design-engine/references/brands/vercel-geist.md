# Vercel Geist
Source: https://vercel.com/geist (design system: colors, typography, components) , https://github.com/vercel/geist-font
Character: Precise, monochrome, engineering-first. High contrast, zero ornament, maximum legibility for technical interfaces.
Poor fit for: playful consumer, hospitality
Palette roles: background `#000000`/`#FFFFFF`, gray-100 `#FFFFFF`, gray-200 `#FAFAFA`, gray-1000 `#0A0A0A`, accent blue `#0070F3`, success `#0070F3`-family, warning amber, error red `#E5484D`; both themes required
Typography: Geist Sans + Geist Mono (OFL, open). Tight display tracking, monospace for technical labels and metadata. Open fallback: Inter, JetBrains Mono.
Shape & space: 6px radius on controls, 12px on large surfaces; 4px spacing base; 1px borders over shadows
Motion: minimal, 100–150ms; instant feedback over animation
Signature moves: pure black/white with one accent, 1px hairline borders, monospace metadata labels, high-contrast type, sharp geometric iconography
Licensing: Geist Sans and Geist Mono are open-source (OFL).
Slop overlap: Inter-adjacent type, monospace labels (deliberate and structural here, not decorative), dark-only for product UI
