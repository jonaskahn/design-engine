# BMW
Source: https://www.bmw.com/ , https://www.bmwgroup.com/ (observable brand/site design)
Character: Premium precision engineering. Confident, technical, understated luxury with strong photography and controlled typography.
Poor fit for: developer docs, social/community
Palette roles: black `#000000`/near-black `#1C1C1C`, white `#FFFFFF`, light gray `#F2F2F2`, accent blue `#1C69D4`-family (BMW blue), muted silver
Typography: BMW Type / BMW Type Next (proprietary, licensed). Open fallback: Inter, Archivo. All-caps section labels with wide tracking; large confident display.
Shape & space: mostly square/low-radius; 4px spacing base; photography fills large panels edge to edge
Motion: slow cinematic crossfades; no bounce, no playfulness
Signature moves: full-bleed dark hero photography, thin uppercase eyebrows, high-contrast type on black, minimal chrome
Licensing: BMW Type font files are proprietary; model names are trademarked.
Slop overlap: dark-only, tracked uppercase labels (deliberate here)
