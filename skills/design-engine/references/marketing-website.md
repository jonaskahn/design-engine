# Marketing Website Branch

Use for product websites, landing pages, lead-generation pages, launch pages, and other conversion-focused experiences. This core hosts four variants for specialized landing-page types: [event-page.md](variants/event-page.md), [nonprofit-donation.md](variants/nonprofit-donation.md), [booking.md](variants/booking.md), and [saas-onboarding.md](variants/saas-onboarding.md) when signup itself is the surface being designed. Read at most one variant alongside this core.

## Required context

Collect only what is missing:

- product or offer and its real capabilities;
- target audience and problem being addressed;
- value proposition or a labeled copy placeholder;
- primary conversion action;
- essential features, process, pricing, FAQ, or other required sections;
- available product visuals and brand assets;
- real proof that may be used, such as approved metrics, logos, testimonials, or case studies.

Never invent claims, outcomes, customers, testimonials, prices, guarantees, or integrations.

## Branch decisions

### Hero format

- Full-screen image or visual background
- Large headline with a product visual
- Minimal oversized headline
- Split product story and product visual
- Motion- or video-led hero

### Hero emphasis

- Value proposition
- Product name
- Brand name
- Product visual or interface preview

### Navigation

- Very simple top navigation
- Transparent floating navigation
- Solid fixed navigation
- Minimal navigation in the hero

### Feature narrative

- Large visual blocks
- Card grid
- Vertical section-by-section explanation
- Alternating left-right sections
- Timeline or process story

### Card usage

- Avoid cards where possible
- Use cards in feature sections
- Use cards only for proof
- Use cards throughout

### Trust evidence

- Verified metrics or outcomes
- Approved customer-logo strip
- Real testimonial cards
- Case-study section
- No social proof available; use a clearly labeled placeholder or omit the section

### Final conversion action

- One primary button
- Lead-capture form
- Direct contact details
- Demo or introductory-call booking
- Continue exploring content before committing

For forms, define the minimum necessary fields and a clear success state. Do not request sensitive information without a product need.

## Output emphasis

The final prompt must establish the conversion path, section sequence, proof strategy, CTA hierarchy, responsive treatment, and placeholder policy. Visual prominence must support the primary conversion action rather than compete with it.
